1. The Consumer Law Deficit

Consumer protection frameworks (CPA 2019, CCPA Greenwashing Guidelines 2024) target only the external marketing statement ex-post. They fail to reach internal board-level decision making, ESG capital allocation, or corporate reporting integrity, necessitating company law interventions.

2. Fusing Art. 21 with § 166(2)

By fusing the constitutional climate right in M.K. Ranjitsinh (2024) with the pluricentric directorial duty under Section 166(2) of the Companies Act (Tata v. Cyrus 2021), the thesis creates an enforceable legal bridge between constitutional environmentalism and corporate law.

3. Overcoming Penalty Arbitrage

Global regulators impose massive monetary deterrents (AUD 12.9M in ASIC v. Vanguard, $5.5M in FTC v. Walmart) while Indian corporate regulators suffer from a zero-penalty deficit. The thesis's proposed Integrated Liability Model cures this penalty arbitrage.
